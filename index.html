03 / Intelligence behind the route

Meet A*,
the route finder.

A* is an informed search algorithm. It prioritizes places that look promising using both known travel cost and estimated remaining distance.

g(n) — the actual cost travelled so far
h(n) — estimated cost to the destination
f(n) — their total estimate
